Questions

What people ask us.

Do I need a PhD?

For most roles, no. A PhD helps for some scientific and modelling roles, but many statistical programming, data management and clinical operations roles start at degree level.

Will I have to take a pay cut?

It depends on where you're coming from and the role. We'll be honest about typical starting salaries before you apply.

Should I do a course first?

Sometimes a short course, such as GCP training or SAS basics, makes a real difference. Talk to us before you pay for anything and we'll tell you what's worth doing for the role you want.
