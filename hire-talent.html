How a search runs

From first call to first day.

  1. Scope the role

    A 30-minute call on the science, the study and the team, not just the job spec.

  2. Search

    We go to our network and to people who aren't actively looking, as well as job boards.

  3. Technical screen

    Each candidate is tested on the work before you see a CV.

  4. Shortlist & interviews

    A short list with notes on each person. We run scheduling and feedback.

  5. Offer & onboarding

    We manage the offer and stay in touch through the first months.
