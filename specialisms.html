Biostatistics

Biostatistics & programming

Statisticians shape a trial from the protocol onward, and programmers turn the data into the tables, listings and figures a submission depends on. A weak hire here shows up at database lock, when it's too late.

Roles we fill

  • Principal and senior biostatistician
  • Statistical programmer (SAS or R)
  • Lead programmer
  • Clinical data scientist

Where they work

  • Phase I to IV trials
  • Integrated summaries for submission
  • Post-hoc and publication analyses

Skills we screen for

SASRCDISC SDTMADaMDefine-XMLICH E9(R1) estimandsSAP writingTLFsAdaptive designsBayesian methods
PK/PD

Pharmacokinetics & modelling

PK and pharmacometrics people decide doses, explain variability and support label claims. It's a small, specialised talent pool, and most good candidates aren't actively looking.

Roles we fill

  • PK scientist
  • Pharmacometrician
  • PopPK/PD modeller
  • PBPK and QSP modeller
  • Clinical pharmacologist

Where they work

  • Preclinical to first-in-human
  • Dose selection in Phase I and II
  • Exposure-response for submission

Skills we screen for

NONMEMMonolixPhoenix WinNonlinNCASimcypPK-SimR / mrgsolveExposure-responseDDI assessment
CDM

Clinical data management

Data managers build the database, chase the queries and get a study to a clean lock on time. When a data manager leaves mid-study, the timeline is usually the first thing to suffer.

Roles we fill

  • Clinical data manager and lead
  • Data coordinator
  • EDC builder and database programmer
  • Medical coder

Where they work

  • Study start-up and database build
  • Conduct and query management
  • Database lock and archiving

Skills we screen for

Medidata RaveVeeva Vault CDMSOracle InFormCDASHMedDRAWHODrugData management plansEdit checks
HEOR

Health economics & market access

Approval isn't the finish line. Health economists and market access teams build the case that payers and HTA bodies need before a medicine reaches patients.

Roles we fill

  • Health economic modeller
  • HEOR manager and lead
  • Real-world evidence analyst
  • Systematic reviewer
  • Market access manager

Where they work

  • Early value assessment in Phase II
  • HTA submissions at launch
  • Real-world evidence after launch

Skills we screen for

Cost-effectiveness modelsBudget impactPartitioned survivalMarkov modelsExcel/VBARNICE STAsEU JCASLR and NMACPRD / HES data
Regulatory

Regulatory affairs

Regulatory specialists plan the route to approval and keep products compliant once they're on the market. Small biotechs often need senior judgement on a contract basis rather than a full team.

Roles we fill

  • Regulatory affairs officer, manager and director
  • CMC regulatory specialist
  • Regulatory and medical writer
  • Publishing specialist
  • Labelling specialist

Where they work

  • Clinical trial applications
  • Marketing authorisation
  • Variations and lifecycle maintenance

Skills we screen for

eCTDMHRAEMAFDACTA / INDMAA / NDAScientific adviceVariationsVeeva RIM
