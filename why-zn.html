Recruitment from inside the industry.

ZN Specialists is a UK recruitment agency for pharma and biotech. We know these roles from the inside and understand what good looks like.

What makes us different

We speak the language before the interview starts.

A generalist recruiter can match keywords. We can tell a SAS programmer from a statistician, and a PopPK modeller from a bioanalyst.

Inside the industry

We know the science and the people doing it. We screen candidates against the work as it's really done, not against a list of keywords.

Fast contractor cover

Small teams can't absorb a gap mid-study. We keep in touch with specialists who are ready to start.

Shortlists, not inbox floods

You get a few people we'd hire ourselves, each with notes on why they fit.

Our story

Proven recruitment, now specialised.

ZN started as a general recruitment agency, and we've built a hiring process that works. [Short history: when ZN started, the sectors and programmes you've recruited for]

We now put that process to work in pharma and biotech. Working closely with the sector showed us how hard it is to find good statisticians, PK/PD modellers, data managers, health economists and regulatory specialists, and how often general recruiters get it wrong.

We focus on smaller biotechs, where one missing person can hold up a whole programme and there's rarely an HR team to run the search.

Who we work with

Biotechs, CROs and pharma teams

We focus on smaller biotechs that need specialist skills without a big HR team behind them. We also support CROs and larger pharma teams with hard-to-fill roles and contract cover.

On the candidate side, we work with experienced contractors, permanent specialists and people moving into the industry for the first time.

How we work

What you can expect from us

  • We learn the science behind a role before we search for it.
  • We screen people on the work, not just their CV.
  • We send short shortlists with notes, not piles of CVs.
  • We're honest with candidates about fit, pay and timing.
  • We never send a CV without the candidate's permission.
